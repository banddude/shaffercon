⚡ CLASSIC HOPSCOTCH
Shaffer Construction

Play traditional hopscotch on the job site!

1. Land on the glowing square to throw your wire nut
2. Hop through all squares except the marked one
3. Reach square 10, then hop back
4. Pick up the wire nut on your way down
5. Return to START to complete the round
6. Complete all 10 rounds to win!

Controls: Arrow keys or on-screen buttons to move & jump
